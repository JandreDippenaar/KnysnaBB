Things to do nearby

Knysna Heads

Twin sandstone cliffs at the lagoon mouth, with lookouts, a cafe and coastal walks.

5.4 km · about 10 min drive View on Google Maps

Knysna Waterfront

Harbourside shops, restaurants and boat cruises, including the ferry to Featherbed.

2.5 km · 5 min drive or 30 min walk View on Google Maps
Photo: Neil Fantom, CC BY 2.0

Thesen Island

Canals, boutiques, oyster bars and restaurants on a former timber-mill island.

3.4 km · about 10 min drive View on Google Maps
Photo: South African Tourism, CC BY 2.0

Garden of Eden

An easy, wheelchair-friendly boardwalk loop through old yellowwood forest.

13.9 km · about 15 min drive View on Google Maps

Brenton-on-Sea

17.4 km · about 25 min drive View on Google Maps
